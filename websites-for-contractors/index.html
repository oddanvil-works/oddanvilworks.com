Questions

I get all my work from word of mouth. Why do I need a website?

Because people who hear your name still look you up before they call. A site with your phone number, services and reviews confirms you're the real deal, and it gives your Google listing somewhere to point.

Do I have to write all the text?

No. You fill in a short questionnaire (services, towns, a few lines about you) and send photos. I shape that into the page. Full copywriting from scratch isn't part of the packages, but most trades don't need it.

Can you fix my Google listing too?

Yes. That's part of the Digital Setup add-on ($150–250), and it's free for the first 5 clients who book by November 30.
