About the studio

Hollis Clay Studio is a one-person pottery studio. Every piece is thrown on the wheel, trimmed by hand, and glazed in small batches, so no two are exactly alike.

Everything is food-safe and dishwasher-safe, and made to be used every day, not kept on a shelf.

Get in touch

Questions about a piece, custom orders, or joining the kiln-opening list: send an email and expect a reply within a couple of days.
