Questions

I already use a booking app. Does that still work?

Yes. The site links to your existing booking page, so your calendar, reminders and deposits stay exactly as they are.

My Google listing points to an old site (or the wrong one). Can you fix that?

Yes. Updating your Google Business Profile is part of the Digital Setup add-on, and it's free for the first 5 clients who book by November 30.

Can I change prices myself?

Yes. Sites are built on Squarespace or WordPress, and you'll get a short walkthrough so you can edit your menu any time.
