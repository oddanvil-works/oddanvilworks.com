Questions

Can I update the menu myself?

Yes. You'll get a short walkthrough at handoff, and changing a price or a special takes a minute.

Can customers order on the site?

The site links to your existing ordering (Square, Toast, DoorDash and so on). Building a full online store isn't part of the packages, but I'm happy to quote it.

I'm a food truck. Is a website worth it?

Yes, if people ever ask where you are today. A schedule section you update from your phone answers that once, for everyone.
