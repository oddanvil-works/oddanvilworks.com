How it works

Four steps, with no surprises about scope or cost.

  1. Quick call

    A 15-minute chat about your project, what pages you need, and your timeline.

  2. Fixed quote

    You get a written scope and price. A 50% deposit books your start date.

  3. Build and review

    You send your text, photos and logo. I build the site and you review it.

  4. Launch

    After final payment, the site goes live on your domain and you get the keys.

Questions

What do I need to have ready?

The text for your pages, any photos and your logo, plus access to your domain. If you don't have a domain yet, I'll help you pick and buy one in your name.

Which platform will my site be built on?

Usually Squarespace or WordPress. Both are established, easy to update yourself, and widely supported. We'll pick the one that fits how you want to manage the site.

Can I update the site myself after launch?

Yes. That's the point of using a standard platform. You'll get a short walkthrough at handoff, and bugs in my work are fixed free for 14 days after launch.

How do payments work?

50% when you book, 50% when the site is ready to launch. Invoices are sent online and can be paid by card. Extra work outside the agreed scope is quoted first, at $75 per hour.

What is the Founding Client offer?

The first 5 clients who book by November 30, 2026 pay the normal price and also get their Google Business Profile set up or cleaned up for free (normally part of the $150 Digital Setup). In return, I ask for a short testimonial, a Google review if you're happy, and permission to show your site under Examples.

Do you only work with businesses in Paducah?

No. I'm based in Paducah, Kentucky, and everything is done remotely, so I can work with you wherever you are.
